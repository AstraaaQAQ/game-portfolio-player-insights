# Narrative Design Demo: The Mine and The Moon

This folder contains public narrative design artifacts for the branching narrative demo.

The main route, **The Mine**, follows Sedes Westly through a mining disaster where every ending tests what he protects under pressure: family leverage, public truth, or solidarity with the miners. The revised A/B ending direction sharpens the cost of survival: one route lets the Westly name survive through a morally compromised bargain, while the other lets the truth survive by turning Sedes and his family into targets.

The side route, **The Moon**, focuses on emotional choice design, asking whether love becomes protection, release, or rebellion.
